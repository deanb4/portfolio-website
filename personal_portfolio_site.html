Room Visualizer

Upload a photo of your kitchen and see it with a different quartz countertop. Clients use it to choose slabs before they buy.

Open live site
  1. photoA client uploads a picture of their room.
  2. segmentA fine-tuned segmentation model finds countertops and other surfaces: about 90% accuracy on a custom set of 2,500+ images annotated in CVAT, trained on A100 and RTX A6000 GPUs.
  3. depthDepth estimation recovers each surface's orientation so the slab sits in perspective.
  4. renderOpenCV and NumPy masks feed a Three.js scene that lays the chosen slab onto each surface.
  5. serveDjango with per-user sessions, PostgreSQL, Redis and S3, deployed on Fly.io; the model runs on RunPod.

Ground Control Station

Live drone telemetry on the desktop

Reads live MAVLink telemetry from an ArduPilot autopilot and draws an instrument panel, GPS flight track and link health in real time.

  • Electron
  • Node.js
  • MAVLink v2
  • UDP
  • SVG
How it's built
  • Decodes position, attitude and system status at stream rate over UDP.
  • The Node main process owns sockets and log files; a sandboxed renderer talks to it through a context-isolated preload API.
  • SVG artificial horizon, heading compass and live flight track.
  • Records sessions to disk and replays them through the same render path; heartbeat timeouts flag a lost link. Tested against ArduPilot SITL.
